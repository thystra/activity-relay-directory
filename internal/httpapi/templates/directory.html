How the relay tiers work

Tiers describe operational confidence, not popularity. Inside each tier, open relays are shown first, then restricted, closed, and unreported relays.

Tier 1 — Heartbeat + Online
These relays send a directory heartbeat and were reachable at the latest check.
Tier 2 — Online, no current heartbeat
These relays are known to the directory and were reachable at the latest check, but do not currently send a directory heartbeat.
Tier 3 — Offline / Unreachable
These relays could not be reached at the latest check but have been seen online within the last 30 days.
Tier 4 — Graveyard
These relays have not been seen online for at least 30 days. They remain listed for historical reference and are checked periodically in case they return.
