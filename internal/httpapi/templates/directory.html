Evidence

Independent directory signals

Heartbeat and server-observed reachability are intentionally separate. One does not rewrite the other.

Heartbeat
healthy through 36 hours; stale after 36 hours and before 7 days; dead from 7 days to before 30 days; prune boundary at 30 days or older. not observed means no retained authenticated Directory observation exists for the entry.
Reachability
reachable means the most recent bounded actor validation succeeded; unreachable means the latest check failed; unknown means no current actor check has been recorded.
Inbox diagnostic
The actor-declared inbox may be responsive, method rejected, unreachable, or not checked. Method rejection does not mean the inbox is absent.
RFC 9421
verified means this directory has accepted a signed lifecycle request from the relay. not verified means only that the directory has no such positive evidence.
