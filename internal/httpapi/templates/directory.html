Find participating relays and see whether they are still sending heartbeats and reachable on the network.

What the statuses mean

Heartbeat and reachability tell you different things about a relay.

Heartbeat
Healthy means the relay sent a recent heartbeat. Stale or Dead means that heartbeat is getting old, even if the relay is still reachable.
Reachability
Reachable means the directory could recently connect to the relay's public actor. Unreachable means the latest check failed. Not checked means there is no recent check to show.
